Selected Course

Lady Bird Lake Trail

Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail

Mixed Terrain

Terrain Summary: A scenic ~13 - 14 mile loop circling Lady Bird Lake in the heart of Downtown Austin. The surface consists primarily of smooth crushed granite, hard-packed fine gravel, and concrete boardwalk sections over the water with minimal elevation gain. Ideal for a relaxed, fast-rolling gravel cruise with skyline views.
